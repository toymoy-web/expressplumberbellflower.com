If You Smell Gas.

Leave First, Call Second.

A rotten-egg smell, a hissing sound near a gas appliance, or a spike on your gas bill with no obvious cause are the signs that bring most gas line calls in Bellflower. If you genuinely suspect a leak, safety comes before anything else on this page.

Leave the Property

Get everyone out immediately if you smell gas strongly or hear hissing near a line or appliance.

Do Not Use Switches or Phones Inside

Avoid anything that could create a spark, including light switches, until you're outside.

Call the Gas Company and Us

Your utility can shut off service at the meter. We handle the repair once it's safe.

Do Not Re-Enter Until Cleared

Wait for confirmation the area is safe before going back inside, entering only once it has been declared safe.

Beyond Emergencies.

Installation and Non-Emergency Repair

Most gas line work isn't an active emergency: running a new line for a range, outdoor fireplace, or pool heater, replacing an aging line during a water heater swap, or repairing a line that failed pressure testing during a home sale. All of this requires a permit and inspection, which we handle as part of the job.

Older Bellflower homes originally built with a single gas appliance, typically the water heater and range, sometimes need a line upsized before adding a second appliance like a pool heater or outdoor fire feature, since the existing line may not carry enough capacity for both.

We size new lines to the manufacturer's stated BTU demand for each appliance being added, not a generic default, which is what actually prevents a pressure problem once everything's connected and running at once.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover gas line repair?

Routine gas line repair or installation is generally not covered, since it's treated as maintenance. Damage from a gas-related incident, like a fire, is a different and typically covered matter, but the line work itself is billed directly.

How fast can you respond to a suspected gas leak?

This is treated as a priority dispatch. Leave the property and call the gas company first, then us.

Do I need a permit for a new gas line?

In most cases, yes, and we handle the permitting and inspection as part of the installation.

Can you install a gas line for an outdoor kitchen or fire feature?

Yes, this is a common request, and we size the line correctly for the appliance's actual BTU demand.

Why did my gas line fail a pressure test during a home sale?

Often an aging fitting or a line that's been slowly leaking at a joint. We can diagnose and repair it before closing.

Can you convert an appliance from electric to gas, or vice versa?

Yes, this typically involves running or capping a gas line and coordinating with an electrician if a circuit also needs to change, which we can help schedule.

How do you actually test a line for a leak versus just guessing?

A pressure test with a gauge, holding the line at a set pressure over time to confirm it doesn't drop, is the real test, not just a soap-bubble check at a fitting.

Are older black iron gas lines a problem, or just outdated looking?

Black iron itself isn't automatically a problem, but fittings and joints on an older line are worth checking, especially if the line has never been pressure tested.

Can a gas leak be too small to smell but still dangerous?

Very small leaks are rare given how strongly the additive scent is designed to be noticeable, but any suspected leak, even a faint smell, is worth calling in rather than dismissing.

Do you handle gas line work for both natural gas and propane systems?

Yes, both fuel types are supported, though the fitting sizes and appliance orifice ratings differ between them and are matched accordingly during installation.

What's involved in capping off a gas line that's no longer needed?

The line is properly capped and pressure-tested to confirm no residual leak, rather than just closing a valve, since a valve alone isn't considered a permanent shutoff.
