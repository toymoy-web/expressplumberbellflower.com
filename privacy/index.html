Privacy Policy

How Bellflower Express Plumbing collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to this website.

Information We Collect.

What We Gather

When you call, email, or fill out a form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, and service address. We also use standard web analytics that may collect anonymous information about how visitors use this site, such as pages viewed and general location.

How We Use It.

Purpose of Collection

Information you provide is used to schedule service, respond to inquiries, and provide the plumbing services you request. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Call recordings, where used for quality and training purposes, are handled consistent with applicable California law.

Third-Party Services.

Analytics and Call Tracking

This site may use third-party analytics and call tracking services to measure marketing performance. These services may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
