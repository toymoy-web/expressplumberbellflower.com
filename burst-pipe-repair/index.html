What to Do Before We Arrive.

A burst pipe doesn't wait for business hours, and neither does the damage it causes. If you're dealing with one right now, the first move matters more than anything else you do before help arrives.

Shut Off the Water

Find the main shutoff valve, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and turn it fully off.

Shut Off Electricity Nearby

If water is near any outlets or panels, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Move Valuables Away

Get furniture and belongings out of the path of standing or spreading water.

Why This Happens Here.

Bellflower's Older Pipe, Under Pressure.

Most burst pipes we respond to in Bellflower involve galvanized supply lines from the original 1950s and 60s construction, corroded thin enough at one point that normal water pressure finally breaks through. A sudden pressure spike, a hard freeze on an exposed exterior line, or simple old age can all be the final trigger on a pipe that's been weakening for years.

The location matters as much as the cause. A break in an exposed garage or crawlspace line is a fast, contained repair. A break inside a wall or under the slab still gets the water shut off immediately, but locating the exact point takes the same detection approach used for a hidden leak.

We carry common pipe fittings and shutoff hardware on the truck specifically because emergency calls don't leave time for a supply run. Most burst pipe repairs are completed in a single visit, water restored the same day it went out.

FAQs.

Common Questions

How fast can you get here for a burst pipe?

Burst pipe calls are treated as a priority dispatch ahead of routine scheduled work, any hour of the day or night.

I can't find my main shutoff valve. What do I do?

Call us immediately and we'll help you locate it over the phone while a technician is on the way.

Is there an extra charge for a night or weekend call?

No, the rate is the rate regardless of when you call.

Will one burst pipe mean the whole line is failing?

Not necessarily, but it's worth checking. We'll assess the rest of the line's condition while we're already there.

What if the burst pipe is inside a wall or under the slab?

We still shut off the water and stop the immediate damage the same way. Locating and repairing the actual break may then need the same detection equipment used for a slab or wall leak.

Should I turn off the main water valve for the whole house or just a section?

If you're not sure which section feeds the break, shutting off the main valve is the safer default. We'll help you sort out zone shutoffs once we're on site so the whole house doesn't need to stay off next time.

Does homeowners insurance typically cover burst pipe damage?

Often, yes, for sudden and accidental damage, though coverage details vary by policy. We can document the break and the repair for your claim, but the coverage decision itself is between you and your insurer.

What if the break happens overnight and no one's home to shut off the water?

An automatic leak detection shutoff valve prevents exactly this scenario by cutting water flow the moment it detects a burst pipe or major leak, worth discussing if this is a recurring worry.
