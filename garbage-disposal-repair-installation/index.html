The Signs.

Humming, Jammed, or Leaking.

A garbage disposal call in Bellflower is usually one of three things. A unit that hums but won't spin, meaning something is jammed inside, is one common cause. A leak at the connection to the sink or dishwasher line is another, and a disposal that's simply reached the end of its working life and needs replacing is the third.

Humming, Not Spinning

Almost always a jam that can be cleared without replacing the unit.

Leaking at the Base

Often a worn seal, which usually means replacement rather than repair.

Leaking at the Dishwasher Line

A loose or failed connection where the dishwasher drain hose attaches.

No Power at All

Could be a tripped reset button, a wiring issue, or a fully failed motor.

A disposal that trips its reset button repeatedly under normal use, not just when something odd goes down the drain, usually points to a motor on its way out rather than a one-off jam.

A leak at the disposal's connection often shares the same fix as a faucet or sink repair nearby. A disposal that keeps jamming despite repair might point to a broader drain cleaning need further down the line.

Repair vs. Replace.

Is It Worth Fixing?

A jam or a tripped reset is almost always worth clearing rather than replacing the unit. A disposal that's leaking from the housing itself, or one that's several years old and showing repeated problems, usually makes more sense to replace, since disposal motors aren't designed to be rebuilt piece by piece.

Fibrous kitchen waste, potato peels, and celery are the most common cause of a real jam in this market, more than anything unusual about Bellflower's plumbing specifically. Running cold water during and after use goes a long way toward preventing it.

Before You Try Anything.

If You're Going to Check It Yourself

Switch It Off First

Always cut power at the wall switch before putting anything near the drain opening, every time, no exceptions.

Never Use Your Hand

Use the hex-key wrench or the reset tool that came with the unit, or a long-handled tool, never fingers.

Skip Chemical Drain Cleaners

They don't clear a mechanical jam and can damage seals and internal components.

Stop If It's Not an Easy Fix

If the jam doesn't clear with the reset tool in a minute or two, that's the point to call rather than force it.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover garbage disposal repair?

No, disposal repair and installation is considered routine maintenance, not damage repair, so it's outside standard coverage. It's billed directly and quoted before any work begins.

My disposal hums but doesn't spin. Is that a big repair?

Usually not. It's almost always a jam that can be manually cleared, often in the same visit without replacing anything.

How long do garbage disposals typically last?

Most units last 8 to 12 years with normal household use. Frequent jams or leaking near the end of that range usually mean it's time to replace rather than keep repairing.

Can you install a disposal I've already purchased?

Yes, we can install a unit you've bought, or supply and install one for you.

Is a leaking dishwasher connection a disposal problem or a dishwasher problem?

It can be either. We check the actual connection point during the visit to confirm which side needs the fix.

Is it safe to try to clear the jam myself first?

Turning off power at the switch and checking for an obvious obstruction is reasonably safe. Using the manual reset tool that comes with most units is fine too, but repeated jamming is worth having looked at professionally.

Do you match the horsepower of my old unit or recommend an upgrade?

We'll tell you honestly whether your household's use justifies a higher horsepower replacement, rather than automatically upselling to the biggest unit available.

What's the most common reason a disposal fails completely rather than just jamming?

Motor burnout from repeated jamming over time, or water intrusion into the motor housing from a slow seal leak that went unnoticed. Both usually mean replacement rather than repair.

Do you offer a warranty on disposal installation?

Yes, both the manufacturer's warranty on the unit and our own labor warranty apply, and we'll go over the specific terms before installation begins.

Can a garbage disposal be added to a kitchen that's never had one?

Usually, yes, provided the sink has the right drain configuration and there's a nearby outlet or the wiring can be run. We'll assess feasibility during the visit rather than assume.

Do you clean up any mess from the old unit or debris in the process?

Yes, cleanup of the work area and disposal of the old unit are included, so you're not left dealing with it afterward.

Is the visit still worth it if the fix turns out to be simple?

Yes, the flat price covers the diagnosis either way, whether it's a quick jam clear or a full replacement.
