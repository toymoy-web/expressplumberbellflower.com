The Housing Stock.

Downey's Building Eras, Plumbing-Wise

Downey incorporated in 1956, and much of its residential base dates to that same postwar tract-home period as Bellflower, meaning galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains are common in the older sections of the city. Newer construction and remodeled homes closer to the Rio Hondo mix in copper and PEX, which changes what kind of call we get depending on which part of Downey a property sits in.

At roughly 114,000 residents, Downey is one of the larger cities in our coverage area, several times the population of Bellflower itself, so calls here span a wider range of neighborhoods and property types than a smaller, more uniform city would.

Downey is also known locally as the home of the original Apollo space program manufacturing site. It's also home to one of the oldest continuously operating McDonald's locations in the world, both reminders that a lot of the city's commercial and residential infrastructure has genuinely been standing since the mid-20th century.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Downey.

Shut Off the Main Valve

If it's safe to reach, turning off the main water valve limits damage immediately.

Cut Power to Wet Areas

If water is near outlets or panels, shut off the breaker for that area if it's safe to do so.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and belongings away from standing water or an active leak.
