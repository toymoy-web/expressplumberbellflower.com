New Installs, Valves, and Conversions.

Shower and tub calls in Bellflower range from a straightforward valve replacement on an aging unit to a full tub-to-shower conversion for a bathroom remodel. We handle the plumbing side of any of these, working alongside a tile or general contractor when the job includes finish work beyond plumbing.

Working With Your Remodel.

Coordinating the Plumbing Side

For a full remodel, we coordinate the rough-in plumbing and valve placement with your tile or general contractor's timeline, so the plumbing work doesn't hold up the rest of the job. For a standalone valve swap or leak repair, it's usually a same-day fix without needing to involve anyone else.

Bellflower's original bathrooms typically shipped with a tub rather than a walk-in shower, which is a big part of why tub-to-shower conversions are one of the more common remodel requests we see in this specific market.

We check the existing waste and supply line locations before quoting a conversion, since moving a drain relative to the original tub position sometimes adds scope beyond the visible fixture swap.

FAQs.

Common Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover shower or bathtub installation?

No, this is a renovation or replacement decision rather than damage repair, so it's not something a standard policy covers. It's billed directly and quoted before installation begins.

Do you do the tile and finish work too, or just plumbing?

Just the plumbing side, rough-in, valve, and connections. We coordinate with your tile or general contractor for the rest.

My shower temperature keeps fluctuating. Is that a valve issue?

Often, yes, especially on an older single-handle valve. Replacement usually resolves it where a repair alone doesn't.

How long does a tub-to-shower conversion take on the plumbing side?

Typically one day for the plumbing rough-in itself, though the full remodel timeline depends on tile and finish work around it.

Can you fix a leak at the shower pan without replacing the whole unit?

Often, yes, if it's a seal or caulking failure rather than a cracked pan. We'll assess before recommending full replacement.

Do you handle ADA-compliant or walk-in tub installations?

Yes, including grab bar rough-in and low-threshold entry configurations for accessibility needs.

Is a tub-to-shower conversion reversible if I sell the house later?

Not without redoing the plumbing rough-in again, so it's worth thinking about resale expectations in your neighborhood before committing to the conversion.

Do you install the shower door and glass, or just the plumbing?

Just the plumbing and valve work. Glass and enclosure installation is typically handled by a specialty glass contractor, which we can coordinate scheduling with.

Can a single-handle valve be swapped for a two-handle style, or is that a bigger job?

Usually a bigger job, since the valve body and trim plate footprint differ between styles. It's doable, just worth pricing as its own scope rather than a quick swap.

Do you handle the demo of an old tub before installing a new shower?

Yes, tub removal is part of a conversion project, along with capping or rerouting the plumbing to match the new shower's configuration.

Is a walk-in shower always a straightforward swap for a tub?

Not always. The drain location often needs to move for proper slope, which is one of the reasons a conversion is priced as its own project rather than treated like a simple fixture swap.

Do you offer a warranty on shower and tub installation labor?

Yes, workmanship is warrantied separately from the fixture manufacturer's own warranty, with terms reviewed before installation begins.

How do you handle a bathroom that's the only one in the house during the work?

Scheduling is planned to minimize how long the bathroom is unusable, and for longer projects we'll talk through options for the household during that window before the work starts.
